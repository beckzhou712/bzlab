I'm Beck Zhou. This site is a working record of the research I'm doing, published
while it's still in progress.

Most research writing is retrospective: you find a result, then you write the
story that leads to it. That story is true but heavily edited — the dead ends,
the reversals, and the weeks spent on an idea that didn't survive contact with
the data all get compressed into a sentence, or dropped. I wanted a place where
that material stays visible.

## How this is organised

Work is grouped into **columns**. A column is one research thread, and its posts
are ordered oldest to newest, so reading a column from the top gives you the
project as it actually unfolded rather than as it would be reconstructed later.
Tags cut across columns for when you want to find everything on one technique.

Each column has its own feed, so you can follow a single thread without
subscribing to the rest.
