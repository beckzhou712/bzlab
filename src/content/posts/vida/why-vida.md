## Why write it down as it happens

Papers report the version of a project that worked. They are written backwards
from the result, which means the interesting part — the wrong turns, the thing
that looked promising for two weeks and wasn't — gets compressed out. That
material is most of the actual work, and it's the part that would have been
useful to read while I was stuck.

So this column is the forward version: written as it happens, in order, without
knowing how it ends.
